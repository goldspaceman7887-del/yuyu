# ゆとりば Reel 001 —「まだ見えていないもの」
### Production package · 15-second vertical Instagram Reel · Black-and-white seinen manga motion

| Item | Spec |
|---|---|
| Working title | まだ見えていないもの (*What I Can't See Yet*) |
| Platform | Instagram Reels |
| Duration | **15.00 s** (360 frames @ 24 fps) |
| Format | 9:16 · 1080 × 1920 px · 24 fps (gives a hand-drawn manga cadence) |
| Audience | Japanese university students and young working adults, 18–35 |
| Central question | 人生には、今見えているもの以上の何かがあるのだろうか。 |
| Voice-over | None, on purpose. The text carries the story, so the reel works fully muted. |
| Text sequences | 5 (plus the ゆとりば wordmark, which works as a logo, not a sentence) |

---

## 1. Final creative concept

On a crowded morning train, Sora (22) is doing everything right: they attend lectures, finish their lists and keep moving. Nothing is wrong, but the manga panels around Sora are always slightly too narrow, and Sora is only ever seen through glass. When the routine stops, the panels open into a single blue-hour splash on a riverside bridge. The page gets wider, the sky takes over the frame, and one honest question gets room to breathe: 「人生は、本当にこれだけだろうか。」 The reel doesn't answer it. A café door opens and the only warm color in the whole piece enters the frame: amber light, a pour of coffee, Aoi looking up, Ren reading, and one empty wooden chair. The final line, 「あなたは、どう思いますか？」, turns the inner monologue toward the viewer. The visual idea is a manga page that keeps getting wider, and Yutoriba is where the page finally has room for you.

**Visual throughline:** panel width = emotional room.
- Scenes 1–2: narrow, crowded, vertical panels
- Scene 3: one panel, large empty space
- Scene 4: no panel border at all (full-bleed splash)
- Scene 5: a warm panel with an empty seat in it

## 3. Shot-by-shot visual descriptions

### SHOT 1A · 0.00–1.25 · "The narrow morning"
A manga page fills the screen from **frame 0**, with no fade from black. There are four panels with thin white gutters:
- **Right, tall panel (≈55% of page width, full height):** a train-door window. Sora's face is reflected faintly in the glass and overlaid on the city blurring past outside. Sora isn't looking at the camera, just slightly past it. Morning light flickers across the reflection. **This is the hook image:** a quiet, tired, intelligent face that's half there.
- **Left column, three stacked panels:** (top) a hand gripping a train strap, with a slight sway; (middle) three commuters lit by their phones, faces in neutral profile, not unfriendly; (bottom) shoes lined up near the doors, mostly leather work shoes, with one pair of worn white canvas sneakers (Sora's).
- The crowd is dense but calm, with ordinary Tokyo-area morning clothes.

### SHOT 1B · 1.25–2.50 · "Eyes in the glass"
The camera pushes into the right-hand reflection panel. The left column slides out of frame by parallax, and the reflection panel grows to full screen. A band of passing light moves across Sora's face from right to left. The push stops on Sora's eyes, which are still reflected, still on the far side of the glass, with a ghost of buildings passing through them. One subtle slow blink.

### SHOT 2A · 2.50–4.00 · "The page assembles itself"
A hard page-turn sound and a short paper whip. Six panels slide onto a white page one after another (≈4 frames apart), like a page being pasted up:
1. **Lecture hall (wide, top band):** Sora seated in a middle row, one of many backs and heads. The professor is a small figure at the front with a projected slide (blank, no readable text).
2. **Ren at a desk (narrow vertical):** an office window grid behind him. Ren types with a straight back, his face composed, with very faint fatigue under the eyes.
3. **Pedestrian crossing (wide, middle):** shot from above, a scramble crossing with dozens of figures moving in subtle parallax layers. **The reel's only speed lines** are a few faint horizontal strokes on the crowd, standing for the pressure to keep moving.
4. **Clock (small square):** a plain station-style analog wall clock.
5. **Checklist (small square):** a hand-ruled to-do list in Sora's notebook with abstract scribble lines instead of readable words. Four boxes are ticked and the last one is empty.
6. **Vending machine (narrow vertical, bottom right):** Sora's hand takes a canned coffee from the slot in a fluorescent glow.

### SHOT 2B · 4.00–5.00 · "Done."
A slight push toward the checklist panel. A pen enters and ticks the final box in one confident ink stroke (frame 101–106). At the same moment the clock panel's minute hand jumps forward one notch (frame 104). Then everything holds still for ~8 frames, which sets up the cut.

### SHOT 3A · 5.00–6.25 · "The panels stop"
**A hard cut, with ambient sound dropping out on the cut.** One single panel shows late evening. Sora sits alone at the window counter of their small apartment, seen from slightly behind and to the side. The window takes up 70% of the frame, with defocused city lights rendered as soft white dots on a solid black sky, and rain trails on the glass. Sora is small in the lower-left third. **Large negative space** sits in the upper right, where the vertical text will go.

### SHOT 3B · 6.25–7.50 · "The hand that stopped"
A close-up insert on the counter: a plain ceramic mug of coffee about half full with a faint thread of steam; Sora's A6 kraft notebook lying open on blank pages; Sora's right hand resting beside it with a pen, not writing. The fingers are relaxed and anatomically correct (five, natural knuckles). A rain trail moves down the reflection on the counter. In the last 6 frames (7.25–7.50), a soft audible breath plays and black ink bleeds outward from the edges, then flips to warm paper white. This is the transition into the splash.

### SHOT 4A · 7.50–11.50 · "The question" (emotional splash page)
**Full-bleed, no panel borders: the first frame in the reel without edges.**
- **Composition (vertical):** the sky fills the top ~62%, with layered stratocumulus clouds drawn in brush ink and gradient tone. The city skyline is a low, detailed band at ~65% height: mid-rise buildings, power lines, a distant train on a viaduct with lit windows. Below that are a quiet river and a pedestrian footbridge rail.
- **Sora** stands at the rail in the lower third, slightly right of center, three-quarter back view with the face in partial profile looking up toward the horizon. The figure is about 16% of frame height: small, but the eye goes straight to it because it's the darkest, sharpest mass. The coat and hair move in the wind.
- **Color:** the sky slowly picks up a muted blue (#5E7891 → #8FA3B5) at top, and a thin warm gold line (#C9A15A) appears along the horizon behind the buildings. Everything else stays ink and tone.
- **Stillness:** for the first 12 frames (7.50–8.00) almost nothing moves. That is the reel's one deliberate moment of stillness.

### SHOT 5A · 11.50–12.25 · "A door"
Cross-dissolve via a vertical light seam. Interior, monochrome: the inside of an independent café's wooden door with a small brass handle and a little bell. The door swings inward and a wedge of **warm amber light** spills across the floorboards. It's the first warm color in the reel, and the ink linework picks up the warmth as it spreads.

### SHOT 5B · 12.25–13.25 · "Someone is already here"
A two-tier page:
- **Top panel (wide, shallow):** pour-over coffee. A gooseneck kettle pours a thin, steady stream into a ceramic dripper over a glass server, with coffee-brown color in the liquid only. The barista's hands are anonymous, with rolled linen sleeves.
- **Bottom panel (larger):** Aoi sits at a wooden table with an open journal and a pen, a small plant in a ceramic pot, warm window light, and a paperback beside her cup. She looks up toward the door with a calm, open face, not a big smile. Behind her and slightly out of focus, Ren sits with his tie loosened, reading a book, a cup in front of him and his work bag at his feet. His shoulders look like they've come down for the first time all day.

### SHOT 5C · 13.25–15.00 · "The empty chair" (final frame)
A wide interior. A window table in the foreground right has **one empty wooden chair** pulled slightly out at an angle toward the camera, as if offered. There's a second cup on the table, and a small brass lamp gives off amber glow. In the left foreground edge, out of focus and cropped, Sora's back and shoulder stand at the entrance in a gray coat: still monochrome, still outside the warmth, but turned toward it. Pale steam rises from the cup. The text sits over the calm upper area and the wordmark sits low in the safe zone. The frame holds for the last 1.25 s.

**Loop note:** Reels loop. The empty chair in 5C cuts back to the crowded train in 1A, so the loop itself restates the contrast between a full schedule and an empty seat that's waiting.

**Reading-time check (phone):** natural Japanese silent reading on a phone runs about 6–8 characters a second.
- T1 has 13 characters on screen for 1.75 s; staging it in two parts makes that work.
- T2 has 13 characters for 2.15 s.
- T3 has 10 characters for 1.9 s.
- T4 has 16 characters for 3.3 s, the longest hold.
- T5 has 12 characters for 2.6 s.

All of these are comfortable.

**Japanese language notes (native-voice review)**
- T1–T4 are plain-form inner monologue (〜ている / 〜だろうか), which is how a 20-something Japanese person thinks to themselves. It isn't declarative or preachy.
- 「ちゃんと」 is intentionally colloquial. It carries both "properly" and "the way I'm expected to", which is exactly the tension the reel is about.
- 「それなのに」 is softer than 「でも」 and carries quiet puzzlement rather than complaint.
- T5 switches to です／ます form. That register change is intentional: the voice stops talking to itself and politely turns to the viewer. It reads as an invitation, not a demand.
- The tagline 「立ち止まって、考えられる場所。」 is **moved out of the video** and into the caption and the Instagram bio. Keeping it on screen would make six text sequences and crowd the last 1.6 s. The final frame shows the question and the wordmark only.

## 7. Character consistency rules

1. **One master sheet per character.** Every frame, by an illustrator or an AI generator, starts from the approved turnaround. Nothing is generated "from description only".
2. **Locked anchors, checked on every frame:**
   - Sora: right-brow hair strand, mole under left mouth corner, stone-gray coat with dropped shoulders, olive bag on the right shoulder, white canvas sneakers
   - Ren: steel watch on the left wrist, 7:3 part, navy tie
   - Aoi: left-ear stud with tucked hair, oatmeal cardigan
3. **No costume changes inside Reel 001.** Sora's coat stays on in every scene, including the café entrance.
4. **Proportions:** realistic adults at 7–7.5 heads. Eye width ≈ one eye-width apart. No enlarged irises or anime highlight shapes.
5. **Line weight:** outer contours are heavier (brush-pen equivalent of ~1.2 pt at print scale) and interior features are lighter (~0.5 pt). Faces get minimal screentone: one soft tone on the shadow side, no airbrush.
6. **Rendering register stays the same across characters:** none of the three may look "more anime" than the others.
7. **Hands:** five fingers, visible knuckle structure, natural nails. Every hand insert (1A strap, 2A vending machine, 2B pen, 3B resting hand) gets a dedicated hand check and manual paint-over if needed.
8. **AI workflow:**
   - Use the same character reference image (Midjourney `--cref` / `--cw 100`, SDXL/Flux IP-Adapter + a small character LoRA trained on the approved sheet, or the reference-image feature of your tool) and keep the style seed fixed per scene.
   - Generate faces at full scale and **inpaint or repaint the face** rather than rerolling the whole frame.
   - A human illustrator does a final cleanup pass on all faces and hands.
9. **Overlay test:** before lock, put every Sora face side by side at the same scale on a review board. If anyone on the team can't tell instantly that it's the same person, that frame goes back.
10. **Pronoun-neutral directions** for Sora in all scripts and briefs, so that no frame drifts toward an unapproved gendered design.

## 9. Motion and transition instructions

**Global rules**
- Motion is **restrained and layered**. Animate parallax layers (separated PSD layers) instead of warping whole images.
- Easing: `Easy Ease` with long tails (in/out influence 70–85%). No overshoot, bounce or elastic.
- Screen-tone drift: dot-tone layers slide 1–2 px/sec in alternating directions. They should be barely visible, but the page feels alive.
- Paper texture and film grain sit on top of everything for the full 15 s (grain at 3–5% opacity, paper at Multiply 15%).
- Hold frames: animate a few key elements "on twos" (12 fps steps) for a hand-drawn cadence. Camera moves stay smooth at 24.

| Shot | Camera | Element motion | Transition OUT |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1A | Static page; whole page gets 1–2 px vertical "train vibration" wiggle (wiggle(6,1.5)) | Strap sways ±2°; phone glow flickers subtly; passing-light band sweeps across the reflection (f8–f26); city layer slides left at constant speed | — (continuous into 1B) |
| 1B | Scale 100% → 260% toward Sora's eyes, centered on the reflection panel; left column exits via parallax | Second light band across face (f40–f52); a single slow blink on twos (f46–f50) | **Page-turn whip** (6 frames, f56–f61): page curls from the right edge, revealing a white page |
