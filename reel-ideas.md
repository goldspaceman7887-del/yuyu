## Room to Breathe (from moodboard: roadside stall)

1. **Hook:** "You don't need a bigger life. You need a place to stop."
2. **20-second script:**
   - (0–3s) Wide, low shot: a tiny coffee cart under a huge summer sky. VO: "Everyone's running."
   - (3–8s) Bike rolls in and stops beside the cart. VO: "But the best moments happen when you stop."
   - (8–14s) Close-ups: pour over, steam, two cups set down, a friend sits. VO: "A small place. A slow coffee. Someone to talk to."
   - (14–20s) Pull back to the wide shot, clouds drifting. VO: "That's *yutori*: room to breathe. Come find yours."
3. **Shot list:** low-angle wide of cart/café front with sky; bike arriving and kickstand down; hands pouring coffee; two cups on the counter; friends laughing (backs to camera); slow pull-back to the wide.
4. **B-roll:** cumulus clouds time-lapse, grass in wind, hanging lanterns or noren swaying, steam rising.
5. **Caption:** Not everything has to be fast. Sometimes the best thing you can do today is stop, sit down, and have a coffee with someone. ☁️☕
6. **CTA:** Tag the friend you'd stop for.
7. **Hashtags:** #yutori #slowliving #japancafe #coffeetime #community #tokyostudents #findyourplace
