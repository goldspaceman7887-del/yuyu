# ゆとりば — Reel Batch 2 (5 reels)

All five use the same world and look as `reel/yutoriba_reel_15s.mp4`:

- Painted cumulus sky, wide green field, red coffee cart with a thatched roof, a teal bicycle and potted plants
- Warm ivory Zen Old Mincho text with a soft shadow, revealed like ink from left to right, placed in the upper middle (y≈430–960)
- Felt-piano soundtrack at 72 BPM with wind and cicadas, no vocals
- 15 s, 1080×1920, ends on the wide shot with **ゆとりば**

To make each reel feel new while staying in the same world, I changed **the time of day or the weather**.

> ⚠️ The verse wording follows 新改訳2017. Check each verse against your Bible before posting.

Suggested posting order: 1 → 2 → 3 → 4 → 5 (introduce the brand first, then the verse, then the three discussion reels).

## 1. 「はじめまして、ゆとりば」 (Discovery) — *afternoon, same as reel 1*

**Hook:** 急ぐ毎日の途中に、こんな場所があったら。 (What if there were a place like this partway through your busy day?)

| Time | Picture | On-screen text | English |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0–3.5 | Low wide shot of empty road through the field. A bicycle rides in from the left, small under a huge cloud. | 急ぐ毎日の、途中に。 | Partway through a busy day… |
| 3.5–6.5 | The rider slows down. The cart appears from behind the grass, and the ひとやすみ banner flutters. Bell rings once. | ひと休みできる場所があったら。 | …what if there were a place to rest? |
| 6.5–9.5 | Close-up of the counter. Coffee is poured and steam rises. The bicycle kickstand goes down (sound). | コーヒーを一杯。 / 人生のこと、心のこと、神さまのこと。 | A cup of coffee. Life, the heart, God. |
| 9.5–12.5 | Steam rises into the clouds and the camera tilts up. | 答えを急がず、一緒に考える。 | Thinking it through together, without rushing to answers. |
| 12.5–15.0 | Wide shot with the bicycle parked beside the cart. | ゆっくり考える場所。 / **ゆとりば** / フォローして、ひと休みしに来てね。 | A place to think slowly. Follow and come rest. |

**Shot list:** bicycle arriving in the wide shot; cart reveal; pour and steam close-up; tilt up into the clouds; closing wide shot.
**B-roll / SFX:** bicycle bell, kickstand click, pour, cicadas.
**Caption:**
急がなくていい場所、つくりました。
コーヒーを片手に、人生のこと、心のこと、神さまのこと。
答えを急がず、ゆっくり一緒に考えませんか。
はじめまして、ゆとりばです。☁️☕
**CTA:** フォローして、ひと休みしに来てね。 (Follow and come rest.)

## 3. 「花火は、なぜ切ないんだろう」 (Japan × life and eternity — Ecclesiastes 3:11) — *summer night, fireworks*

**Hook:** きれいなものほど、すぐ消えるのはなぜ？ (Why do the most beautiful things disappear so fast?)
**Angle:** もののあわれ, the Japanese feeling for passing beauty. We all feel loss when beauty fades, which hints that our hearts were made for something that lasts.

| Time | Picture | On-screen text | English |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0–3.0 | Night: deep indigo sky with the clouds as dark silhouettes. One firework blooms far beyond the field. Delayed boom. | きれいなものほど、/ すぐに消えていく。 | The more beautiful something is, the faster it fades. |
| 3.0–6.0 | Two more fireworks. Two cups and a 風鈴 (wind chime) on the cart. | 花火も、夏も、/ 大切な時間も。 | Fireworks, summer, precious moments. |
| 6.0–8.5 | The last spark fades and the sky goes quiet. Only the wind chime is heard. | なのに、なぜ / 「ずっと続いてほしい」と思うんだろう。 | So why do we wish they'd last forever? |
| 8.5–12.0 | The stars come out slowly across the whole sky. | 「神はまた、人の心に / 永遠を与えられた。」/ 伝道者の書 3:11 | "He has also set eternity in the human heart." |
| 12.0–15.0 | Wide shot of the cart under the stars with the lantern glowing. | その切なさは、/ 永遠を知っている心のしるしかも。/ **ゆとりば** | That ache may be a sign your heart knows eternity. |

**Shot list:** first firework over the field; cart with cups and wind chime; fading spark; stars filling the sky; starlit closing wide shot.
**B-roll / SFX:** firework booms (distant, soft), wind chime, crickets.
**Caption:**
花火が消えたあとの、あの静けさ。
切なくなるのは、心が「終わらないもの」を知っているからかもしれない。
「神はまた、人の心に永遠を与えられた。」（伝道者の書 3:11）
あなたは、どう思う？🎆
**CTA:** あなたが「ずっと続いてほしい」と思う瞬間は？コメントで教えて。 (What moment do you wish could last forever? Tell us in the comments.)
**Hashtags:** #ゆとりば #花火 #もののあわれ #夏の終わり #永遠 #伝道者の書 #聖書 #人生について

## 4. 「雨宿りの縁」 (Japan × connection — Genesis 2:18) — *sudden summer shower (夕立)*

**Hook:** フォロワーは何百人。でも、本音を話せる人は？ (Hundreds of followers, but who can you really talk to?)
**Angle:** 縁 and 一期一会, the Japanese ideas of connection and of chance meetings. People were designed for each other.

| Time | Picture | On-screen text | English |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0–3.0 | Grey, heavy cumulus. A phone lies on the counter, its screen glowing with notifications (no readable text). | つながってるはずなのに、/ なんでさみしいんだろう。 | We're supposed to be connected, so why are we lonely? |
| 3.0–6.0 | 夕立: sudden rain across the field. Two bicycles hurry in and park under the thatched roof. Shown from the back only. | — (rain sound carries the beat) | |
| 6.0–9.0 | Close-up: two cups are set side by side, steam mixes, raindrops fall from the roof edge. | 雨宿りで、/ はじめて話した。 | Sheltering from the rain, we talked for the first time. |
| 9.0–12.0 | The rain stops. Light breaks through and a rainbow appears over the field. | 「人がひとりでいるのは良くない。」/ 創世記 2:18 | "It is not good for the man to be alone." |
| 12.0–15.0 | Wet, shining wide shot with two bicycles parked side by side. | 人は、つながるように造られた。/ **ゆとりば** | We were made for connection. |

**Shot list:** grey sky with the phone glow; rain sweeping across the field; two bikes arriving; two cups in the rain; the sun breaking through with a rainbow; closing wide shot with two bikes.
**B-roll / SFX:** rain on thatch, distant thunder (soft), dripping, birds return after the rain.
**Caption:**
画面の中には、たくさんの人。

「人がひとりでいるのは良くない。」（創世記 2:18）

**CTA:** 最近ゆっくり話せてない友だちを、タグ付けしよう。 (Tag a friend you haven't really talked with lately.)

## 5. 「あなたは、作品」 (Japan × human design and God — Ephesians 2:10) — *early morning*

**Hook:** 比べて、比べて、疲れてない？ (Tired of comparing yourself again and again?)
**Angle:** 職人 craftsmanship. A handmade cup always has a maker who made it on purpose. So do you.

| Time | Picture | On-screen text | English |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0–3.0 | Dawn: pale peach sky, mist over the field, the cart still shuttered. | 比べて、比べて、/ 疲れてない？ | Comparing and comparing, aren't you tired? |
| 3.0–6.0 | The shutter lifts. A row of handmade cups on the shelf, each slightly different. | 同じ形の器は、/ ひとつもない。 | No two handmade cups are the same. |
| 6.0–9.0 | Close-up: a cup is turned to reveal the maker's stamp (a small 印) on its base, and a sunbeam hits it. | ていねいに作られたものには、/ 必ず作り手がいる。 | Anything carefully made has a maker. |
| 9.0–12.0 | The cup is filled and steams in the morning light. The camera tilts up to the sun rising over the clouds. | 「私たちは神の作品であって」/ エペソ人への手紙 2:10 | "For we are God's workmanship." |
| 12.0–15.0 | Bright morning wide shot with the mist lifting. | あなたも、目的をもって造られた。/ **ゆとりば** | You too were made with a purpose. |

**Shot list:** misty dawn wide shot; shutter opening; row of different cups; maker's stamp in a sunbeam; pour and tilt up to the sunrise; closing wide shot.
**B-roll / SFX:** morning birds, shutter rattle, ceramic clink, kettle.
**Caption:**
職人の器は、ひとつひとつ違う。
それは失敗じゃなくて、作り手のしるし。
「私たちは神の作品であって」（エペソ 2:10）
あなたも、目的をもって造られた作品。🌅
**CTA:** 保存して、自分と比べてしまう日に見返してね。 (Save this for the days you compare yourself.)
**Hashtags:** #ゆとりば #職人 #うつわ #自分らしく #比べない #エペソ人への手紙 #聖書 #朝活

## Production notes

  - a sky/lighting preset (afternoon, sunset, night, rain, dawn)
  - two or three new props (lantern, wind chime, fireworks, rain, cups with a maker's stamp)
  - its own text timeline
- Keep the same last-frame layout as reel 1 so the five covers line up on the grid.
